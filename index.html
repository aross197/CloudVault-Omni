How BlackBerry Optimized Storage

  • Content Compression — enabled by default on classic devices to shrink stored data.
  • Best-fit allocation — data was written into the tightest free flash blocks to reduce fragmentation.
  • Keep free space high — low free flash caused long garbage-collection pauses; the goal was always ≥10–15% free.
  • Reduce media resolution — lower camera/image sizes before storage.

CloudVault Omni applies the same principles to free cloud tiers: compress hard, then route to the provider that currently has the most free space.
